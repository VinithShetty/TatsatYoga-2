Core Philosophy

Yoga is not about perfection. It is about presence.

At its heart, yoga is a practice of returning — to the breath, to the body, and ultimately, to the self. It creates space to slow down, listen inward and cultivate a deeper sense of awareness.

Tat Sat Yoga embraces the idea that every body has its own rhythm and every journey its own pace. Through movement, breath and stillness, the practice nurtures strength, flexibility, balance and inner calm — both on and beyond the mat.

A practice not to become more, but to become more aware of what already is.

Tat Sat Yoga is an invitation to slow down, breathe deeply and meet yourself where you are.
I hope to walk beside you on this path of yoga — one breath, one movement and one moment of awareness at a time.

My Story

I don’t think I chose yoga. I think yoga chose me.

My journey began not as a carefully planned destination, but as a quiet unfolding. What started as a practice slowly became a way of understanding myself — my body, my breath, my thoughts, and the spaces in between.

I am a 300-hour certified yoga teacher, trained at Vinyasa Yogashram, Rishikesh, with several years of experience teaching yoga. My practice and teaching draw from Hatha, Vinyasa and Yin Yoga, bringing together movement, breath, stillness and awareness.

About my teaching:

At the heart of my teaching is a simple belief: yoga is not about perfect postures; it is about coming home to yourself. It is a practice of listening rather than forcing, of building strength while learning softness, and of creating a deeper connection between body and mind.

My classes are beginner-friendly and thoughtfully adapted to individual needs — whether your intention is to build strength and flexibility, support weight management, improve focus, manage stress, or simply create a little more balance in everyday life.

Services & Offerings

Hatha Yoga

A traditional and mindful approach to yoga that combines asana, breath and awareness. Hatha builds strength, stability, flexibility and body awareness while creating a steady, grounded practice.

Vinyasa Yoga

A dynamic, flowing practice where movement and breath move together. Vinyasa builds strength, mobility, balance and stamina while creating a moving meditation through mindful sequences.

Yin Yoga

A slow, meditative practice focused on deep relaxation, mobility and releasing tension. Postures are held for longer periods, allowing the body to gently open while cultivating patience, stillness and awareness.

Pranayama(breathwork)

Conscious breathing practices that help develop breath awareness, nervous-system regulation and mental clarity. Suitable techniques are incorporated to support relaxation, focus, energy and emotional balance.

Meditation

Guided practices that encourage stillness, presence and inner awareness, helping create greater calm and clarity in everyday life.

Strength & Flexibility Training

Yoga-based movement and functional strength practices are designed to improve muscular strength, joint mobility, flexibility, balance and overall physical resilience.

Movement for a Sedentary Lifestyle

Targeted practices to counter the effects of prolonged sitting and inactivity, with a focus on common concerns such as back pain, neck discomfort, shoulder stiffness, tight hips and restricted mobility.

Women's Health & Hormonal Wellness

Gentle, supportive practices designed to promote movement, relaxation and body awareness through different stages of a woman's life, with a focus on supporting overall wellbeing and hormonal balance.

Senior Citizens Chair Yoga:
A gentle, accessible practice designed to improve mobility, balance, flexibility and strength while promoting relaxation and overall well-being. The session uses a chair for support, making yoga safe and comfortable for seniors, including those with limited mobility or difficulty getting down to the floor.
